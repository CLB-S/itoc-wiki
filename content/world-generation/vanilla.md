# Vanilla World Generator

Pre-generated world with realistic rivers and mountain ranges:

![](/content/world-generation/vanilla/map.png)

Real-time world generation after pre-generation:

![](/content/world-generation/vanilla/world.png)

Currently, ITOC vanilla world has a default pre-generated size of 100km x 100km.

![100km x 100km](/content/world-generation/vanilla/vanilla-world-size.png)

The entire pre-generation time is less than 4 seconds. (i5-12500h)

![](/content/world-generation/vanilla/vanilla-world-gen-0.png)

The rivers and mountains are generated by solving _the stream power equation_, see [G. Cordonnier et al., “Large Scale Terrain Generation from Tectonic Uplift and Fluvial Erosion”](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/cgf.12820).

The uplift map is generated from plate tectonics:

![](/content/world-generation/vanilla/vanilla-world-gen-1.png)

The uplift map which controls the river generation and height map generation:

![](/content/world-generation/vanilla/vanilla-world-gen-2.png)
